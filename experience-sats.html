Creating a purpose-driven brand narrative and integrated talent attraction campaign for Asia's gateway and food service provider.

Singapore Airport Terminal Services

  2. The brief
  3. Our approach
  4. What we delivered
  5. The result

Overview

SATS is Asia's gateway and food service provider, keeping airports, airlines and kitchens running across the region. As the business grew, the story it told prospective employees had not kept pace with the work itself.

We worked with SATS to define a purpose-driven brand narrative and bring it to life in an integrated talent attraction campaign, from research and narrative development through to the touchpoints candidates meet first.

The brief

SATS needed to attract and keep talent in a tight market, where frontline roles compete with every other service industry. Candidates knew the name but not the work, and the roles they saw rarely showed the scale and craft behind them.

The brief was to give people a clear reason to join, and a story current employees would recognise as true.

Our approach

We started by listening. Interviews and workshops with leaders and frontline teams across ground handling, cargo and food solutions surfaced what people valued most about the work: the pride of keeping Asia connected and fed.

From there we shaped a narrative around that purpose, then tested it with employees and candidates before taking it into the campaign.

What we delivered

A brand narrative and messaging framework, an employee value proposition, and a campaign toolkit covering the careers site, social, recruitment events and on-site environments.

Every piece was written so that hiring managers and their teams could use it without us in the room.

The result

The narrative now anchors how SATS describes itself to candidates and to its own people, giving recruitment and internal communications one shared story to work from.
